Your seven categories

USA Pickleball rates players across seven categories. Every measurement we have is here — and each one says what it is actually worth, so you can see where the effort pays. Categories are ordered by their biggest available gain, not alphabetically.

Reading the “worth” column

It answers one question: if you improved this one thing by ten points and changed nothing else, how far would your overall rating move? A row marked — would not move it — and the reason matters a great deal, so every such row says which of these it is.

What to do about it

You get to the line, but you and your partner are up there together under a third of the rally — and one of you back is an invitation through the middle. Your paddle also sits as low at the net as it does at the baseline; that costs you reaction time even though it does not move the number.

▸Get-to-the-line: after every return, sprint to the kitchen line and freeze before the next ball.
▸Move as a unit: shadow your partner across the kitchen keeping 8–10 ft spacing; close the middle together.
▸Paddle up & ready: between shots, hold the paddle up in front around chest height, not down at your side.
